Additionally, for every 15 feet of movement, the attacking creature's AC decreases by 1.*

		- *They are at the same place that they were in Avandra's ruined temple: at the altar*

# We Escape
- We are dead silent as we walk through the interdimensional corridor
- Vallen and Cordelia ask where Quén went during their banishment -- they were in the chapel where they always meet with their goddess, but she wasn't there
	- Cordelia theorizes that they prayed to Lathander, which is why <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">The Sun</span> card was pulled. They didn't though: they prayed to Avandra and Camille
	- Julian: Lathander is quiet. He was not there. That Sun was *you*
- Quén messages [[The Keeper|V]] to let him know that we're back and have news for him. He instructs us to meet him upstairs immediately
- Cordelia and Kaya realize that - never in their lives has the weather in the Capital been gloomy before, due to the protective magic around its walls - but right now, the sky is overcast
- Pushing our way towards the elevator, we notice that people are rushing around, in and out of Purah's office (etc.etc.)
	- In the elevator, we briefly talk about how we weren't prepared and how we essentially failed
	- Cordelia shares that she believes something is terribly wrong, but she doesn't know what it is
- [[Ozwin]] and [[The Keeper|V]] are discussing things in the Keeper's office, and turn around to greet us

# Meeting with The Keeper
- V requests that we tell him everything that happened
	- Cordelia says that we probably need a recap from him as much as he needs one from us, but explains EVERYTHING that happened (excluding that we met/communicated with [[FAFO]] prior to the battle)
	- Cal tells V all about the egg on the battlefield
- The Keeper informs us that all magic has been redirected to the outer walls, and that **we only have 3 days left**
	- The Keeper has killed [[Sulk]], who revealed that [[The Father]] has a master plan known as the *prophecy*
	- Part of the prophecy is that OSIRIS will summon an army and attack the Clover Capital in 3 days' time
		- There is more of the prophecy that has to do with us -- V knows of this part already
- **V tells us that we can ask for whatever we need - regardless of price - but he will need us on the front lines**
	- Quen requests that Cal, Vallen, and Julian get Mithril armor
- Cordelia asks about the integrity of the inner city
	- The lower levels - the sewer systems - have been fully blocked off - caved in by the [[Earth Faction]] - since they are *infested with Warforged*
- V asks us if we know where the title of "Keeper" comes from
	- The Orb has been in Clover's possession at a few points in history (the Pyramid as well, but with less frequency)
	- The Keeper's ultimate task - other than governance - *is to keep [[Shapes of Paracausality|The Shapes]]*
		- Keepers are chosen through rite and trial, and are granted the ability to handle 
	- **V BELIEVES THE FATHER IS A KEEPER**
		- "Keeper" might not be the term that the Father uses, and V doesn't know how the Father might be a Keeper
		- V can utilize the Orb's power himself, and can handle it without harm
		- "To use the Orb is to push a big red button on biological life exploding. To use the Pyramid is the exact opposite"
- V theorizes that the Father is trying to "trick fate", and use True Changelings to create "Seekers" of his own that take on our identities
- V has mobilized all factions
- We have 3 days total. V warns us to speak with our families beforehand
- Cal spins around, getting in Vallen's face
	- **Cal berates Vallen for pulling him out of the battle, saying that we could have won that and prevented all this from happening**
	- Quén bristles, and both they and Vallen point out that OSIRIS nearly killed Cal. Before more can be said, Cal turns and leaves
