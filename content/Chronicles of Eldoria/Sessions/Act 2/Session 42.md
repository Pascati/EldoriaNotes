- We sit at our usual table (Vallen joins us as well!

- Ozwin talks about the Raven Queen, and how - outside of Eldoria - she can be found in the <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Shadowfell</span>, which is a purgatory of sorts

	- Ozwin posits that **the Father is trying to kill the gods that have sided with the Eldorians**
- TL;DR:
