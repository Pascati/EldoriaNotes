# Talking to Aratish
- Kaya leaves the war room - and her friends - behind, going towards her mother's room to talk to her about our plans
	- Quén casts <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Guidance</span> on her before she leaves
- Kaya tells Aratish that - as warriors and "saviors of this land" - that we need to face the body before the head. She emphasizes that we have a lot to learn and can do a lot to help people by aiming for lesser targets (i.e. the Warforged) than jumping straight into the fire
	- She's shaking as she tells her mother this
- Aratish: *I'd be lying if I said I didn't expect this.*
	- She doesn't know what will happen, but she says that she trusts Kaya if she thinks this is the right decision to make
	- She iterates that Kaya's making difficult decisions, and that this is "befitting" of her, since she will one day be queen
		- Kaya says that she hopes she will be ready one day, but isn't ready now
- Aratish asks where we plan to go, but stops herself and says that she doesn't need to know
	- She trusts that Kaya will return eventually
	- Aratish will remain in the Capital and try to keep Alexios from following Kaya
- Aratish asks if there's anything we need
	- She says that she can spare some gold and rations, but not so much in the way of weapons
	- She's willing to give Kaya **3,000 gold** (for any fees that we run into) and **2 weeks of rations**, which will be sent to the Eldorian ship right away
	- Aratish also offers a **sending stone**, and asks Kaya to write to her
- Aratish says that she can't leave the capital, and stands up with her arms out (in that she offers Kaya a hug)
	- Kaya hugs her! It feels genuine :]
