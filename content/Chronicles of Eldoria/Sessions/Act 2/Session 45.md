	-  Crouches down and readies himself (charging up something?)
- Kaya

	- As the light from the sword ripples out through the air, everyone gains **15 temp HP**
	- They throw the sword, spinning it through the air Alucard-style, and deals ~~10~~ 20 points of radiant damage to Ralnor (doubled because radiant and he's undead)
- Cordelia
	- Pulls <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">The Hermit</span> as a bonus action, which enables her to cast <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Confusion</span> on a creature of her choice (she chooses Ralnor, who succeeds 😔)

	- He deals 20 damage WITH A GUN!!

## Round 2
- Ralnor
	- His sword - still pointed at Kaya - glows red (originating from his hand, rather than stopping at the hilt of the sword) and fires off four blasts of fire at Kaya
		- Kaya casts <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Shield</span> in reaction to this
		- 3 hit, dealing 14 damage to her
	- Descends from his throne, and stops at the bottom of the stairs
- Vallen
	- In one quick motion, he springs forward and passes Ralnor, slashing him with his sword as he passes, dealing 11 damage
- Kaya
	- Walks up to one of the nearby streams - next to a large boulder - holding her hands out to
	- *"Hey, stream. I know we really don't like this guy right now, and that we kinda have a vendetta against him - since you killed his sister before - do you wanna do something similar to him? Please?"*
		- Nothing happens 😔
		- Insight: 15. She feels like this is a trial, and recalls the phrase she's been hearing for weeks: *"You don't command the tide, you move with it."*
		- She realizes that **the water is moving in a very specific direction** (away from Ralnor?)
	- Stomps on the ground, taking an earthbending stance and moving the boulder she's standing next to
		- The boulder blocks the stream, redirecting it across the room and towards the other stream at the other side of the room
- Quén
	- Walks up to Cordelia and casts <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Warding Bond</span> on her
		- As they put a hand on her shoulder and give her a reassuring squeeze, Cordelia - for a split second - looks to her side and expects to see Camille there
	- Rolls a NAT 20 to hit Ralnor with their <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Spiritual Weapon</span>
		- Deals 15 (doubled to 30 bcs Ralnor's undead) radiant damage
- Cordelia
	- Uses a bonus action to pull <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">The Chariot</span>
		- This enables herself or an ally to automatically reroll a Nat 1
	- Uses an action to fire another <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Thunderwave</span> at the wall next to the waterfall
		- It doesn't hit EXACTLY where she wants it to, but still manages to break open the wall a little more to allow more water to pour in
- Cal
	- Runs and dashes forward, jumping over one of the streams of water and stopping directly in front of Ralnor
	- He attacks twice, missing both times
	- He whispers to Ralnor: ***"I fucked your sister"***

## Round 3
- Ralnor
	- His sword glows light blue, and as it does it seems to generate some wind force around it
		- Attacks Cal with a 34 TO HIT
		- Deals 32 points of damage to Cal, pushing him back 10 feet
	- **CAL CASTS <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">HELLISH REBUKE</span> AS A REACTION**
		- Deals 7 damage to Ralnor
	- Cal makes a DEX save
		- Quén uses their reaction to spend a hit die and give Cal a +6
		- *Cal throws up :( and he refuses to admit that he did it*
		- He manages to stay upright/not get pushed prone
	- Flings the rocks from Kaya's earlier casting of <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Erupting Earth</span> at Kaya, but they seem to phase right through her and harmlessly hit the wall behind her
- Vallen
	- Reaches out a hand, and space begins to distort around Ralnor. A beam of light forms between him and Ralnor (it's Vallen's version of <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Lightning Bolt</span>), dealing 25 points of damage
- Kaya
	- Pushes the second boulder into the second stream, splitting it in two -- it merges with the original path of the stream, with the other half meets with the stream that cuts across the center of the room
	- Moves to be in front of the middle stream that cuts across the center of the room
- Quén
	- Moves their full movement to stand next to Cal, placing a hand on him and standing in waist-deep water with him
	- Heals Cal with <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Cure Wounds</span> for 8 HP
	- Attacks with their <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Spiritual Weapon</span> (20 to attack) and misses -- Ralnor ducks and the sword whizzes by his head
- Cordelia
	- Floats 20 feet off the ground as she pulls <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">The Moon</span> card as a bonus action (she will safely land on the ground at the start of her next turn)
	- Fires the <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Spellslinger</span> at the waterfall, PERFECTLY hitting her shot (with a NAT 20) and widening the hole even further -- the waterfall is GUSHING atp
		- **Both pillars near Ralnor's throne begin to crumble**
- Cal
	- Still standing in waist-deep water, which is **slowly rising**
	- Lays on his back in the water, lazy river-ing down the stream
		- As he does so, he pulls out his guns and fires at Ralnor
		- Rolls a Nat 1, which triggers Cordelia's <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">The Chariot</span> effect -- he manages to hit with a 28. The other shots miss

## Round 4

	- Forces Kaya to roll a DEX save (26)
	- The earth beneath Kaya's feet begins to shift, and the section of ground she's standing on tilts sideways so that it's diagonally perpendicular to the ground

		- Forces everyone to make a DEX save

	- Uses their <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Channel Divinity</span> without expending a hit die or a charge of Channel Divinity, and as they do they feel their heart pound harder for a brief moment

		- We all have to make DEX saves (we all pass, but still take 8 points of damage)
