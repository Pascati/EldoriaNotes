# Combat Incoming
- At this point, it's obvious that the changeling ship is gunning for us. **It is 100 feet away.**
- **SHIP INFO**
	- Our ship is a man-o-war, a 3-mast ship
	- The Changeling ship is a galleon, a 2-mast ship
- Upon Captain Seamus's command - and calling everyone on deck - people make their way to their stations/on deck and begin lowering the sails
	- **We have cannons**
- BEFORE WE ROLL FOR INITATIVE:
	- Cal tells Cordelia that - as much as he knows that she loves to burn things down - that might not be wise on a wooden ship. To that end, he tosses her the <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Spellslinger</span> and a children's book of first-level spells
		- The <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Spellslinger</span> is a stringless crossbow that allows her to load the entire book into it. For every page in the spellbook, she can cast <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Thunderwave</span> as many times as there are pages in the spellbook
			- She can also create her own spellbooks preloaded with OTHER spells 👀
			- *The spellbook currently has **20 pages***
		- Normal crossbow range is 80/320. Because <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Thunderwave</span> is a from self spell, **minimum range 40 and maximum range is 160**
- Quén casts <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Death Ward</span> on Cordelia and <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Water Walk</span> on the following people:
	- Quén
	- Cordelia
	- Kaya
	- Cal
	- Ozwin
	- Captain Seamus
	- Kade
	- Vallen

	- Holds his action?

## Round 2

	- There are **12 figures** currently visible on deck

- Cal
	- Forgoes his action to stand on the edge of the ship - one leg up on the wall - and take a swig from a bottle of rum
	- THE ENEMY ROLLS A NAT 20 ATTACK ON HIM
		- A PISTOL SHOT RINGS OUT. THEY LITERALLY SHATTER THE BOTTLE IN CAL'S HAND
	- Perception (24): Cal sees *himself*, wearing his exact clothes, ALSO holding a bottle of rum, and with his leg up just like Cal is currently standing
		- The other Cal puts his pistol away and casts <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Eldritch Blast</span>, hitting two people on our ship with two bolts
		- One person doubles over in pain, and another one falls over, dead
- Quén
	- Jumps down and casts <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Revivify</span> on the person who died, telling them to get below-deck and protect themself
	- Uses their movement to re-climb the middle mast
- Vallen
	- Grabs Cal and teleports to the other ship (space seems to stretch and bend around them as they do this)
		- Cal throws up
		- They're surrounded by roughly a dozen pirates, one of which looks exactly like him
	- **Decapitates a pirate!!!** LET'S GO KING (Lily comes)
- Cordelia
	- Passive Perception: 15 (she doesn't see anything)
	- Casts <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Firebolt</span> on the enemy sails and deals 2 fire damage to the sails
		- An ember catches on the sails, and they begin to ignite
	- <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Invisibility</span> goes away :(
- Kaya
	- Gets a running start and jumps onto the enemy ship!
	- She sees:
		- The enemies prepping the cannons to fire
		- Cal and Vallen standing on deck, surrounded by enemies (and one dead body)
	- Yells to the Fogstrider that the enemy is starting to load their cannons

## Round 3

	- Runs to the wheel and drops the enemy's anchor (this will take a full round to take effect)
	- Uses his ring to call out to Quén, asking them to tell Captain Seamus to pull the ship in front of the enemy ship's
- False Cal
	- Dips into the shadows and <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Shadowsteps</span> to be directly next to Cal
	- He tosses the bottle of rum, grabs a sickle, and slashes across Cal's left arm for 11 damage
	- Casts <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Eldritch Blast</span> on Kaya, dealing 13 damage to her
		- Cal sees this and gets MAD (his neck twitches again)
		- He thinks to himself: *I can't let them attack my friends.*
- Enemy 1
	- Attacks Vallen (who manages to deflect)
- Enemy 2
	- Runs up to Kaya
- Enemy 3
	- Prepares attack with bow
- Enemy 4
	- Prepares attack with bow
- Enemy 5
	- <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Charms</span> Kaya
- Quén
	- Passive Perception (19): **THEY SEE THE BLUE MAN**
		- He's in the crow's nest, looking around and drawing something in his notebook
	- They use <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Control Water: Flood</span> to create a 20-foot wall of water that slams into the ship, rocking it and (hopefully) making the cannons less likely to hit their shots
	- They call to Kade and toss him a <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Potion of Greater Healing</span>, telling him to make sure nobody gets hurt
		- Immediately after that, they jump to the other ship (NAT 20 ATHLETICS) AND LAND USING AN ENEMY CHANGELING AS A CUSHION. THEY SKID TO A STOP RIGHT IN FRONT OF THE ENEMY SHIP'S MAST

- Cordelia
	- Asks Captain Seamus if the cannons are ready (they are): *"Then ready them."*
	- Holds her action to cast <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Enlarge/Reduce</span> on a cannonball once they fire

		- She doesn't see any unrefined materials that she can use her abilities on :(

- The enemy ship is also *starting to sink*, with some water visible on the stairs leading belowdecks

- Cal
	- Dips down to grab the bottle of rum. As he stands back up, he transforms into **Erin Thorne**, breaking the bottle over False Cal's head. *"Pay attention to me."*
		- He deals 4 damage with the bottle!
- False Cal
	- In response to Cal's attack, he locks eyes with him and pulls out his pistol, using it to FIRE TWO SHOTS AT KAYA
		- Kaya casts <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Shield</span>, raising her AC to 22
		- Only one shot is able to tear through her shield, dealing 22 damage
	- He says *"I'm better than you now."* at Cal, who currently looks like Erin Thorne

		- Because there's no longer as many supports beneath the deck, the thing holding the anchor up (I don't know the name of it 💀) collapses and falls into the ocean, leaving behind a MASSIVE HOLE and also no more anchor
	- She yells at Cal to *"GET OUT OF THE FUCKING WAY!"* and casts <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Fireball</span> on the middle of the ship (which she has a clear line of sight on because of the enlarged cannonball)
		- She specifically wants False Cal to be caught in the blast, since he's drenched in alcohol

# Back on the Fogstrider
- It is raining.
- Back on the Fogstrider, we watch the enemy ship fully sink. A few remaining Changelings are left in the water, still alive
- PERCEPTION
	- All the people we killed float to the surface, and their bodies change from various races to all being Changelings
	- **We do not see the body of False Cal OR the Blue Man.**
- Kade is off to the side, feeding healing potions to injured crew members
- Cordelia asks Quén how they knew the ship was full of Changelings
	- Before they can fully answer, Vallen advises us to go somewhere where it isn't raining

## In the Bar
- We sit at a table in the corner, and Quén casts <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Silence</span> on a majority of the room so we can speak in relative privacy
	- As we settle in, we hear Captain Seamus bragging to the crew about how he was able to whip the ship around and his ship driving skills
- What Quén shares
	- The Father spoke to them, and said that he offered the pieces of the Shapes 
- Cal shares that the Diamond army has always aimed to make itself stronger, either by incorporating vampires or machinery.
- Vallen tells us that the story is that **Ihys is dead, and has been since the beginning of time**
	- Apparently, there were two cosmic beings: one embodying the concept of good (Iyes) and the other the concept of evil (Asmodeus)
	- Iyhs made the Material Plane, after which Asmodeus killed her
	- As punishment for killing Ihys, Asmodeus was sent to Nessus (the ninth level of hell)
		- The other gods decided to send Asmodeus to Nessus
		- Asmodeus can also *not leave Nessus*
- Quén theorizes that the Father wants to reverse Asmodeus's imprisonment
- Cal wonders if we can contact Asmodeus
	- The gang is like D:
- Asmodeus can still sway people on the Material Plane through proxies
	- Kaya realizes that the Father might be a proxy for Asmodeus
- **We theorize about ways to tell each other apart and how to tell Changelings apart from us**
	- We agree to have a "call and response" type deal to check if we're Changelings:
		- *"Are you goonalooning"*
		- *"My chances of an Eiffel tower are slim but not none."*
	- We need to figure out what Changelings/True Changelings are weak to (kinda like how Vallen's allergic to garlic)
	- False Cal was wearing a ring like Cal's, but *the insignia was wrong*
		- Almost as if it was misinterpreted because it was viewed from a distance.... 🤔 (looking at you, Blue Man 🫵👁️👁️)
		- Quén suggests that we *turn our rings around* (so the design faces our palms and not outward) going forward
	- Quén tells the party that they have a spell that they can cast (<span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Hallow</span>) that can prevent Fey and Undead (among other creature types) form entering a small, specific space
		- Vallen's like ?? but we won't be able to enter it?
		- Quén says that it could be a safe space for the crew to retreat to, or we could use it to test any new members or untrustworthy members of the crew
		- Vallen suggests that we get an enchanter to put a similar spell over the stairs leading belowdecks, and to get himself and Quén passes so they can still pass through
- Quén tells the party that they don't trust Kade
	- *"His laissez-faire attitude doesn't inspire much confidence in the strength of his loyalty"*
	- They argue that, if he's willing to keep things from us for the sake of his agenda or someone else's, they can't trust him with information regarding our hunt for the Shapes
	- Kaya suggests that we allow Kade to speak and try and earn our trust. Quén agrees.
		- Vallen and Cal are of the mind that we can just kill him 💀
	- The gang agrees that we can figure out what Changelings might be weak to, and test it out on Kade
