### Quén looks for a blacksmith
- It is pouring rain
- They walk up to a warforged cleaning up their forge (under a canopy)
- They ask for weapons made of "Axio Steel"
	- The warforged seems to freeze, despite its face being emotionless
- The warforged asks how they learned about the name
	- They say that they were meditating on a way to fight against the true changelings, and it came to them in a vision of sorts
	- Upon hearing this, their body language relaxes
- They're willing to part with a piece of Axio Steel, but implore Quén to **not let FAFO know**, since FAFO is the one who oversees Axio Steel
	- They dip into their shack, locking the door behind them. A few moments later, they emerge with a single dagger
- *Item get! Axio Steel Dagger*
- Quén ventures into the woods and tests the dagger on themself by cutting their palm open
	- It burns in the same way that silver would burn a vampire
	- The wound turns necrotic, and dark ichor drips from it and sizzles on the ground
	- They hiss, saying *"This is good, but feels bad"*. As they walk back into the town, they squeeze their wounded fist tight, focusing on the pain rather than on their deal with The Father

	- It is currently in the late afternoon
- Our ship is out of the way (so the enemy will choose either to chase us or head to Parallax Reach) but isn't entirely hidden. The enemy most likely sees us 💀
- Cal pulls out his periscope (used in the fight against False Cal) and tries to look at the enemy ship
	- Quén uses their cleric ability to give Cal a +1d8
	- He sees someone in the crow's nest, seeming to hold a periscope

	- **We're able to hide 2 warforged before the smokescreen clears**
- Cal looks back at the ship, and notices:
	- Two figures standing at the front of the ship (one smaller than the other)
	- People busy running around the ship
	- Where their canons would normally be - the holes are boarded up. So they're unable to engage in long-range combat at the moment
- **What is our game plan?**
	- Draw them into conversation (lure them into a false sense of security)
