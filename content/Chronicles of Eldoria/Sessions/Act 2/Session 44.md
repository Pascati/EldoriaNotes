# The Trial
## Entering the Trial Chamber
- We enter a dark tunnel, at the end of which is an ornate stone door
	- Next to the stone door is a pedestal with a button with a **very rusty spike** in the middle
	- Quén asks Opal why Darius tried to press the button when it's so clearly dangerous
		- She responds by saying that - after guarding something for centuries but not being allowed to look deeper into it - one gets curious
- Kaya VERY CASUALLY puts her hand on the button and presses it (with her "fuck it we ball" mentality that she's been rolling with lately)
	- The spike pierces the center of her palm and goes *all the way through the other side*
	- As the spike passes through her hand, gold blood runs like a river down the spike and onto the button
- The door swings open!!
	- Beyond the door is a **dark, rectangular room with a wet floor and dripping water**
	- Opal peers inside, and when asked says that her curiosity has been sated. She's also **sworn an oath** saying that she cannot set foot past the door, but at least she's seen it now!
	- Quén tells her that Kaya can tell her all about the Water Trial when we come back, even if she can't come with us
- Opal asks if we have a camera -- we do!
	- Quén hands V's <span style="color:rgb(197, 124, 22)">Camera Stone</span> to Kaya
	- We learn how to use one! We just have to *say the person's name before taking a picture*
		- To this end, Kaya gets Opal's full name 😳 Her name is **Opal Joyreis**
- We enter the hallway, and as the door swings closed, Opal calls out a final warning to us:
	- *"Oh, if you run into any memories of Ralnor, don't mention his sister!"*

## The Trial Begins (Trial 1)
- What we see as the doors close:
	- Torches burst to life on either side of the room, illuminating it
	- Water drips from the ceiling
	- On the other side of the rectangular room is another door with an inscription on it
		- It's a **VERY ANCIENT**, **VERY WET** door
		- The door appears to be locked
		- The inscription appears to be ornate pictures with no real meaning to them, but it resembles the *flowing of water*
- Kaya attempts to push the door, and it doesn't work
	- When she tries, she hears a voice in her head *"You don't command the tide, you move with it."*
	- The whole room begins to shake
- Only Cal sees that the ceiling is beginning to part, and water is flowing down the walls
- Quén tries to help Kaya work out how her powers work as the water level beings to rise
- Quén sees that the runes on the door have patterns and buttons
	- The patterns:
		- The bottommost pattern is a **crashing wave**
		- The second pattern is a **plateau**
		- The third pattern is **fire**
		- The fourth pattern is **wind**
	- The buttons:
		- Each pattern includes two buttons on both doors
- Quén suggests to Kaya that she manipulates the water to press the buttons
	- First, she tries to press them with her hands -- doing so makes the water flow faster
- Kaya holds out her hand to the water, attempting to ask it what it wants to do
	- Doing so makes it flow faster
- Any other suggestions that we make cause the water to flow faster
	- i.e. using earthbending to close the gap above us
- The water continues to rise, and eventually rises higher than our heads
	- Before Quén goes under, the last thing they say to Kaya is *"I trust you."*
- The gang makes CON saves!
	- Kaya rolls the lowest, and thus loses breath the fastest
	- As the breath leaves her lungs, she instinctively breathes in and realizes that **she can breathe underwater**
- The runes on the door glow, and Kaya swims over and presses all the buttons in order
	- As she does so, the water recedes and the door opens!

## Trial 2
- The next room is circular
	- There is water - perfectly still - that leads up the wall and into a cavern on the other side of the room. It's like a curtain of water
- As Kaya steps in it, she realizes that the pool is extremely shallow
- The rest of the party approaches the curtain with Kaya, and as Cordelia and Quén lock eyes with their reflection they freeze and become entranced
	- Cal also sees himself, but manages to avoid being entranced by his reflection. However, as he looks at Kaya, he finds himself being unable to move of speak
- As Kaya looks at her reflection, she sees it begin to change
	- The first vision shows her with a crown on her head, Thraxxus by her side. A city aflame in front of her, a tyrant over her kingdom
	- The second shows her abandoning her friends in exchange for a quiet life
	- The third shows her dying to save her friends, but failing to save the world
	- The final shows her refusing responsibility to Solmara - refusing the crown - and in return the crown falls into someone else's hands and the world turns to despair
	- Kaya's reflection speaks to her, and tells her *"Choose a possible future."*
- *"I am worldshaper, and I will shape my own future"*
	- The reflection attempts to tell her that she's wrong, but Kaya argues that this is the Water Trial and that water is prone to change
	- The reflection argues that water acknowledges, but does not get the opportunity to choose
> [!INFO] "Water does not choose but it acknowledges. A river knows exactly where every rock is and adapts to them. A river recognises a mountain and it can cut it in half or go around it. Water does not have fear of these obstacles. Water reacts to these obstacles without fear. Fear isn't a weakness, refusal to act is."
- Kaya asks if the possible futures she was shown are the "obstacles" she is to react to
	- The reflection does not respond
	- *"I guess we'll just see what happens"*
		- The reflection smiles, and the water curtain parts
- The rest of the party comes to, and Kaya lets us know that the second trial has been passed
	- She tells us that the lesson here was that she just needs to acknowledge the challenges in her path, and adapt to take them as they come
	- Quén responds by saying "as Kaya would"

## Trial 3
- Beyond the door of the next room is a massive column of water - one which must be passed through in order to pass through the room itself - with a number of objects frozen in it
	- The party sees:
		- A shield (closest to the door)
		- A sword
		- A cobblestone brick
		- A burnt gauntlet
		- A feather (eagle's)
		- A map
- Kaya attempts to stick her hand in the water
	- The water does **not feel wet**
	- When she pulls her hand out, it's **completely dry**
- Quén sticks their finger in, and it comes out dry as well
- The party agrees to follow Kaya's lead, since everything she's tried - without our suggestion or guidance - has worked (and it's her trial, anyways)
- Kaya enters the water, swimming towards the closest item: **the shield**. As she grabs it, she *sees a vision*
	- She sees - from a third-person perspective - an elven man with the same shield on his left arm
	- A child walks up to the man, and says *"Ralnor, thank you so much for saving the town"*. As they say this, they hug his leg
	- Ralnor smiles, and a tear runs down his cheek
	- As the vision leaves her mind, the shield dissipates into bubbles
- **The sword**
	- She sees Ralnor again, this time with a sword and the previously-seen shield
	- He runs up a hill, leaping and spearing a red dragon through the head with the sword
	- As the dragon lays dead, he turns and looks at a town in the distance -- she recognizes it as the same town as the one the child was from
- **The cobblestone brick**
	- It is day. It looks eerily similar to Langston. A smaller elven boy - the same colored hair as Ralnor (younger Ralnor) - gets pushed down by a older kid
	- The older kid moves to push Ralnor's friend to the ground, and Ralnor rises to his feet and stands up for his friend

> [!INFO] CJ clarifies that he thinks this campaign passes the Bechdel Test

- **The burned gauntlet**
	- Ralnor is in his early to mid-twenties in the midst of a war. He's fighting off what looks like dark elves (drow) in an unfamiliar place. It appears to be in Silvanour (based on the trees around him)
	- Ralnor raises a gauntleted hand and raises a ring of fire around him. He stomps his foot and the earth moves in response, launching enemies into the air
	- He appears to be locked in on a specific individual as the battle rages on: a dark elf man with dark skin and white hair
	- Ralnor raises his gauntlet towards the target, and with tears in his eyes, he spares the man's life.
- **The eagle's feather**
	- Ralnor is sitting and regretting his life choices in a *prison*
		- He's specifically in a *sky cell*
		- His cell is a room carved into the side of a cliff, with empty space in front of it
	- He sighs, gets to his feet, and leaps into the empty space outside his cell, flying away
- **The war map**
	- Ralnor appears to be in his mid-thirties at this point, and is a highly-decorated war general
	- Ralnor appears to have lost the spark in his eye that he had fighting as a world-shaper in previous visions
	- He plans an attack. He stands at a table *surrounded by dark elves*
		- **Kaya does NOT suspect that he is gay for the dark elves. But we all have our suspicions 😏**
- As the final item bubbles away, the water begins to recede. Kaya's feet touch the ground as the water disappears
	- Laying on the ground in front of her is a seashell necklace
- **The seashell necklace**
	- Ralnor is sitting on a beach, next to his little sister.
	- His little sister stands, running into the water. She reaches into the waves and picks up a seashell
	- He takes the seashell, looking down on it. As he looks up, the vision changes, and he's watching her scream *as she drowns*
		- She's drowning in a cave, and Kaya sees him trying to pull the water apart - away from his sister - and she hears the same voice that spoke to her -- first with Thraxxus, then in the first trial:
			- *"You do not command the tide, you move with it."*
			- *"I have mastered the other three, obey me and bring my sister back!"*
			- *"You do not command the tide, you move with it."*
- The party asks Kaya what happened
	- She tells us that she got a lot of information about Ralnor - one of the previous world shapers - and she doesn't believe the trial ended well for him.
	- She tells us exactly what she saw (she exposits all over us)
- A door on the other side of the cave entrance opens up
- Kaya says that we (as in the rest of the party) are cooked if she doesn't save us
	- Quén reiterates that they trust her
	- She says that their trust means a lot, and moves towards the next room

## Trial 4
- The next path meanders somewhat before it opens up into another cavern
	- At the center of the cavern is a throne, atop of which is a **man who looks VERY dead**
	- He's leaned back, mouth open
- As we walk up to the throne, we see a necklace: the seashell necklace Ralnor had, with the main shell *cracked down the middle*
	- The room shakes as we approach
	- Ralnor's dead body wheezes, leaning forward. Water runs down his face from his gaping eye sockets and mouth
- *"Another worldshaper... Leave while you can."*
	- Kaya responds, saying that she can't do that
	- *"I lost everything, and you will too. I have become the very thing that I sought to control. How many will die from your mistakes throughout your life? What is strength?"*
	- Kaya responds by saying that strength is power
- Ralnor's body reaches down, grabbing the same sword that Kaya saw in her visions. He holds it at the ready, and asks Kaya *"What would you sacrifice to save the world?"*
	- *"Myself, my sanity, everything I have."*
- Ralnor picks up his shield, equipping it. He asks her: *"And how many lives are acceptable to lose?"*
	- *"I can only prevent so much, but I will save anyone I can."*
- Ralnor's corpse chuckles. *"You speak like a worldshaper. But you have no idea what you say. I will end your pain, and I will save you from the deep."*
